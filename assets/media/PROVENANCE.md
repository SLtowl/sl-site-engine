# Promotional media provenance

The repository cover is standalone OpenAI imagegen artwork showing floating website plaques in a deep, dark scene. It is not a browser capture. The depicted interfaces are illustrative artwork, not executable examples of the skill's output. Generated text and invented brands are not real business claims.

The eight-second film animates the approved scene in `motion/public/accepted-scene.png` using camera transforms and layered plaque-reveal masks in Remotion. It is a promotional animation, not interactive websites or automatic page scrolling.

The full MP4 contains 480 frames at 60 FPS, 1920 × 1080, with no audio. The lighter GIF preview is 12 FPS. Technical metadata is in [sl-site-engine.json](sl-site-engine.json). The cover, film and preview are preserved independently of demonstration galleries.
