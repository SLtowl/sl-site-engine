# Demo media provenance

The repository cover is standalone OpenAI `imagegen` promotional artwork, not a browser capture. It shows floating website plaques in a deep, dark scene. The artwork was iterated to remove the earlier mechanical-generator concept and include a text-led education design. These depicted interfaces are illustrative artwork, not executable examples of the skill's output. Generated text and invented brands within the artwork should not be treated as real business claims.

The current eight-second promotional film uses `motion/public/accepted-scene.png`, camera transforms and layered plaque-reveal masks in Remotion. It contains 480 frames at 60 FPS, without audio. It is a promotional animation of the generated scene, not interactive websites or automatic page scrolling. The lighter GIF preview is 12 FPS. Exact technical metadata is in `assets/media/sl-site-engine.json`. Superseded mechanism and individual-site textures are not needed by this composition and are excluded from the public snapshot.

The RUNE 01 example is a fictional product. Its interface, copy, and typography are rendered from HTML and CSS. The three subject assets were generated with OpenAI `imagegen` for this repository, then used as ordinary local files in every browser render.

- `media/rune-product.png` — isolated transparent product render.
- `media/rune-room.png` — architectural room scene generated from the product render as an identity reference.
- `media/rune-detail.png` — macro material study generated from the same identity reference.

The source prompt defines a compact graphite spatial speaker with a black glass aperture, a machined silver rim, a woven acoustic base, and one amber status light. Follow-up prompts preserve that object while changing only the scene and crop. No interface, label, headline, or button was generated inside the images.

The demo makes no claim that RUNE 01 is a real product or that its visible form has working acoustic engineering.
